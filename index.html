Hazardous Materials

We do not remove, transport, or dispose of hazardous materials — including chemicals, solvents, pesticides, asbestos, or biohazard waste.

Paint, Fuel & Chemicals

Latex or oil-based paint, fuel canisters, propane tanks, and solvents must be taken to a licensed household hazardous waste facility.

Heavy Construction Debris

We do not haul drywall, concrete, roofing materials, tile, or heavy demolition debris from active construction sites.

Biohazard & Medical Waste

Sharps, medical waste, and biohazardous materials require certified professionals. We will not handle these under any circumstances.

Plumbing, Electrical & Structural Work

We are not licensed contractors. We don't do repairs, wiring, plumbing, HVAC, or work requiring a trade license.

Guaranteed Same-Day Without Availability

We do our best but will never promise same-day service we can't deliver. If we can't make it work, we'll tell you honestly.

FAQ

Are you licensed and insured?+
Yes. Iron Path Services is fully insured. When we step onto your property, you're working with a legitimate, protected business.
How does pricing work? Are there hidden fees?+
Never. We provide a transparent, itemized price in writing before any work begins. What we quote is what you pay. See our full Pricing page for every rate and fee.
How does the deposit and payment process work?+
Once you approve the quote and sign a simple contract, we collect a 50% deposit to lock in your date. The remaining 50% is due after job completion and your approval. We take before and after photos on every job. No approval, no final payment.
What payment methods do you accept?+
Cash, check, credit/debit card (via QuickBooks), Venmo, PayPal, and Cash App. All methods available for deposits and final payments.
What items do you NOT remove?+
We do not accept hazardous chemicals, paint, fuel, biohazard waste, asbestos, or heavy construction debris. Not sure about an item? Just ask — we'll give you a straight answer fast.
Do you offer same-day service?+
Often yes, depending on availability. Same-day priority is +$75. Contact us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate. We never promise same-day service we can't deliver.
How far do you travel?+
Winthrop and Twisp are included at no extra charge. Mazama/Carlton +$25. Brewster/Pateros +$45. Beyond 30 miles from Winthrop, $2.25/mile one-way. All travel fees disclosed upfront at booking.
How does the vending machine service work?+
We supply, install, stock, maintain, and repair everything. No upfront cost to you. Your location earns 30% of net sales — fully passive income. We operate on a standard 12-month agreement with flexible terms.
